## Appearance review

The two frontal pruning scars are now occluded, the crown reads narrower, and
the right scaffold branch overlaps the central trunk. These are useful side-view
cues missing from the [earlier rejected drawing](root-oak-view-090-rejected.png).
The olive/umber foliage and bark remain compatible with the original palette.

This does not verify an exact 90-degree turn. The image alone cannot establish
which rear scaffold branches are newly exposed or whether their attachment
heights correspond to the original. The visible high crown, lower fork and
root flare need a common anatomical interpretation across opposite views.
Lighting and apparent ground contact also require registration comparison.

## Acceptance still needed

  pruning scars and buttresses across the original and both side views.
- Check crown volume, branch continuity and root location with the

- Register all accepted views to one world scale and root pivot, then compare
  adjacent headings at normal and strategic in-game zoom.
- Derive corresponding worked, low and depleted frames only after accepting
  intact directions. Four stock states at one angle are not four directions.
