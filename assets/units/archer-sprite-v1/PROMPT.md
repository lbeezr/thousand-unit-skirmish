# Image-generation prompt

The source prompt requested original painterly cutout art for the Archer kit: a transparent atlas with eight fixed-camera oblique facings and six aligned rows. Each cell was to show the same slender archer in a moss hood and muted tunic, with a wood bow, a visible back quiver, lightweight boots, and a sky-blue Azure sash as the only vivid team accent. The rows were idle, two walk poses, draw, release/recovery, and defeated. No target, projectile trail, scene props, or other characters were requested.

The two walk rows can be previewed as a short loop; draw/release can be played as a short attack sequence. Facing order and action continuity remain approximate until each row is inspected and corrected.
