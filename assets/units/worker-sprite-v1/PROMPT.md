# Image-generation prompt

The source prompt requested original painterly cutout art for the Worker kit: a transparent atlas with eight fixed-camera oblique facings and six aligned rows. Every cell was to show the same short worker with a warm ochre cap, muted tunic, visible brown leather backpack, broad wooden hand tool, boots, and an Azure-blue sash as the only vivid team accent. The rows were idle, left-foot walk, passing/right-foot walk, a wood-gathering chop, a two-handed build strike, and defeated. No scene props or other characters were requested.

The two walk rows can be previewed as a short loop. Gather and build are single pose samples, not temporal animations. Facing order remains approximate until each row is inspected and corrected.
