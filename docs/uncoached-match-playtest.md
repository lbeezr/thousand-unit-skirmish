## One repeatable session

Use a fresh ordinary room on an identified containing release and deployment, both desktop browsers, unchanged Terraced Vale160 `skirmish@1`, ordinary opening/paid costs and fog. Record source/served/deployment identities before and after, browser/device/viewport, seat assignment and solo policy/map seeds if applicable. Stop acceptance if the build changes. The invite and existing access are supplied through the authorized session path; keep credentials/reclaim tokens out of observations and captures.

Allow 30 minutes of play plus five minutes of questions, announced before starting. This is a session time budget, **not a game deadline**. Give only: “Play this match as you normally would. You can say what you are trying to do. I will observe without explaining the controls.” Supply the URL/invite and existing access; use the shipped brief/labels without additional hints or a hidden observer setup. Log help requests verbatim and answer “What would you try next?” once without suggesting an action. If stuck, observe for up to two minutes, then record a blocked step; rescue only to recover a broken session, recording exact intervention and excluding affected actions from unassisted acceptance. Participants may stop at any time.

Observe the first two minutes: can each newcomer identify their team, the Skirmish objective, an actionable Worker/army, a route and how to issue an order? During ordinary play record the first gather/deposit, building choice and placement, completed producer/recruit, enemy contact and post contest. Do not instruct a build order. Record which visible alternative they considered and why. For each naturally failed command note expected action, actual feedback, whether they understood the reason and their next step; do not manufacture a failure or teach the fix during this opening.

At an actual result, let players find the winner/reason, recap and next step without prompts. Record whether they distinguish remaining bank from total income, survivors from losses and bonus posts from victory. Observe host rematch and guest waiting, then usable orders. If time expires with a living hidden survivor or an open match, record **ongoing at session stop**, survivor knowledge from each player's lawful view, continued meaningful options and any request to resign/draw. Do not invent a winner, reveal enemy positions, force surrender, or run an hour-long cleanup to obtain a success.

Ask afterwards: “What were you trying to do?”, “Why did you choose that building?”, “What happened when an order failed, and what could you do next?”, “What changed the match most?”, “What does the recap tell you, and what is missing?”, “What would you do now?” Preserve player explanations separately from observer interpretation and server facts. Do not label a player's claimed decisive moment as an authoritative event. Record no-results as no-results.

Only after the uncoached opening/result observation, optionally perform the existing reload/ten-second disconnect/reclaim check with disclosure and log it as an instructed recovery task. Swap seats and repeat with the same source/map. An initial pair finds blockers; two additional newcomer pairs can test recurrence once the user authorizes them. Small samples establish observations, not a population success rate or balance win rate. Fix the first repeatable coaching dependency in a focused PR and rerun that step on an identified containing build.

Prioritize inability to act/finish/recover, then repeated misunderstood denial/building choice, then recap/next-step confusion. A native reproduction closes source behavior only. Release inclusion, identified delivery, rendered appearance and human acceptance each retain their own evidence/status.
