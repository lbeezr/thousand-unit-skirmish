# Restrained young-hornbeam pockets — 1 October 2026

`forestAges=pockets` selects seeded four-cell regeneration patches and replaces
65% of hornbeams in the selected 30% of patches with authored young forms.
Other species keep their normal size, and retained mature hornbeams keep theirs.
Current Rootways has 67 young and 295 mature hornbeams, versus 362 mature in
uniform mode; all other species counts and 1,026 wood owners remain unchanged.

Actual isolated renderer checks hornbeam stock 6/3/1/0/6 with expected atlas
rectangles, finite transforms, loaded textures and unchanged map. Reset pixels
are identical, syntax passes, no reported capture errors. Inspected candidate
retains substantially more mature canopy than the earlier young mix; root detail
and repeated anatomy persist. Full-game density review remains before adoption.
