# Underbough canopy crowding diagnosis — 1 October 2026

The fixture renders the same intact Root Oak at the same root, scale, flip and yaw
through its lifecycle atlas and its individual source texture. Both close images
were inspected and show the same intact canopy and roots. The recorded atlas
rectangle matches the full-state frame (allowing Float32 storage precision).
This rules out a wrong-frame selection for this representative Root Oak case;
it does not certify every species, stock state or live match.

The second pair restores all Rootways trees at stock six and compares their usual
scales with a disposable cohort study: one mature-size tree per two-by-two cell
group, the others at 42% of their original scale. Both were inspected. The study
separates silhouettes but reveals a regular arrangement and additional prominent
roots; it is not accepted as a runtime improvement. The camera shows a local
forest edge, not the complete map. Ground and fog are intentionally absent to
isolate sprite overlap. All source map JSON remains unchanged and scales are
restored after capture. The browser completed without errors.

The next composition attempt needs irregular spacing/age groups and less busy
mature-tree silhouettes, while preserving visible resource ownership. Do not
promote the study's square cohort rule or silently hide blocked wood cells.
This is visual diagnosis, not gameplay, hosted or performance evidence.
