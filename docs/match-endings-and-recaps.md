## Audit before changing rules

At source `13b13e82`, `server.mjs` already provides recovery-aware elimination, terminal winner/reason, an authoritative elapsed clock, checkpoint restore, per-seat bank/alive counts and public post ownership. `src/main.js` already displays a result, disables orders and offers host rematch/guest waiting. There is no player resignation or mutually accepted draw command, automatic inactivity draw, or durable general income/spending/loss/history ledger. Bannerfall's persisted kills are its military evolution credit, not all casualties. Unit IDs can be reused with generations; dead rows and final roster differences cannot reconstruct losses. Scenario trigger activation is not a complete or causal battle history.

The server already resolves simultaneous Bannerfall stronghold destruction as a draw. The client has a draw-detail branch for this reason but excludes it from accepted draw reasons, hiding the result. Fixing that display restores the existing contract; it changes no termination rule.

Two different retained cases must stay separate:

- [Pacing PR372](https://github.com/lbeezr/thousand-unit-skirmish/pull/372), measurement source `24144f6b`: same-policy paid Tiny mirror, one elimination at 2,393 seconds (39:53); the reversed seeds leave one Worker alive at 3,600 seconds, ongoing rather than a draw. No policy or route cause is established by this receipt.
- AI [PR371](https://github.com/lbeezr/thousand-unit-skirmish/pull/371)/[PR379](https://github.com/lbeezr/thousand-unit-skirmish/pull/379), [Scout evidence](qa-pve-scout-completion-2026-10-04.md): legal hidden survival. [PR390 contact memory](qa-pve-contact-memory-2026-10-04.md) reaches a last publicly disclosed coordinate with ten-second expiry, but neither retained comparison rediscloses/damages the survivor or establishes improved full-game completion.

Neither case demonstrates that a hidden unit is illegitimate or that bank/army advantage should silently award victory. A Worker can hide and rebuild; a Scout can evade. Route failure, legitimate hiding and inability to recover are different findings.

For a future resignation contract: only the current connected seat may confirm its own resignation with matching match identity; no spectator/host-for-guest authority. Rejected, stale and repeated commands change nothing. Resolve resignations at one tick boundary with existing terminal results winning precedence; simultaneous resignations draw. A draw offer is visible to both seats, expires after a disclosed 60 seconds, may be declined/withdrawn, and requires both current human seats in the same match. Disconnect is neither resignation nor acceptance; no automatic AI surrender in this slice. Rematch clears pending offers. Accepted endings persist their reason/elapsed/result and stop simulation consistently. Save pending-offer expiry on the authoritative clock or explicitly cancel pending offers on restore; never resurrect an accepted offer. New wire commands, reason allowlists, checkpoint validation/migrations and result labels need mode/runtime/lobby agreement and two-seat native proof before implementation.

Product choices needed: choose voluntary endings versus a separate bounded variant; approve which explicit modes expose the commands; select pending-offer restore behavior. For a bounded variant also choose duration, tie handling, what score measures and its weighting, and whether revealing those scores during play respects fog. No threshold, hidden-resource score, survivor revelation or automatic draw is authorized by this note. Do not shorten or weaken the existing 3,600-second test to claim completion.

## Smallest integrated feedback slice

Use only the accepted terminal state, never browser-observed deltas. The result card keeps winner/reason and existing rematch controls; a collapsed **Match recap** shows elapsed time, the viewing player's final Food/Wood (Stone when present), living Workers/other units and held posts out of the received post count. Label bank as remaining resources, not income. Label posts as posts, not percentage territory; Skirmish posts are bonuses. Missing/invalid fields show unavailable, never zero. Spectators receive duration but no player economy/roster/ownership recap. Enemy resource values and hidden enemy rows are never consumed, even if an unfogged DTO contains them.

Income, total spending, casualties and earlier turning points remain explicitly unavailable. The terminal reason is the established ending event, not a claim about the strategically decisive moment. No extra entity scan on the server, per-tick histories, network field, telemetry service, persistent browser storage or checkpoint schema is needed. Rendering scans at most the already received terminal roster/posts. Result reconnect/late join rebuilds from the same frozen saved authority; accepted rematch clears the recap. A victory notification alone can display the result but must wait for an accepted terminal snapshot before reporting totals.

For a later losses/income slice, first agree definitions and bounded authoritative counters at actual deposit/payment/death hooks. Distinguish grants/refunds/repair from gathered income, military/Worker/civilian losses, generation reuse and cancellations. Persist completeness/coverage and counters; old checkpoints retain unknown history rather than claiming totals since match start. Keep fields seat-private through server DTO construction. A bounded event list should describe actual recorded events without claiming causal importance. This is a dependent proposal, not invented instrumentation in the first slice.
