## Bounded source behavior

An accepted plain-node Gather installs version-one Gather with Food and required
`sourceKind: 'neutral-land-food'`, original anchor and Worker generation. Existing
Wood/Stone/construction record shapes, checkpoint schema 29/rules 6 and public
unit rows remain unchanged. Only living owned reachable explicitly selected
Workers receive that job. Neutral sources stay neutral; no worker recruitment,
new inventory, stock reservation, regrowth, payment or tuning is added.

On depletion, choose positive finite plain Food inside the original eight-world-
unit circle, currently visible and actually reachable. Distance from the Worker
then stable source ID determines preference. Compatible partial cargo fills
across nodes; full or incompatible cargo uses existing owned compatible depots.
Accepted external/manual/queued orders retain priority and rejected/stale/foreign
commands preserve complete authority. Natural area exhaustion returns the final
load and clears the job without anchor expansion or hidden-resource wake-up.

Farm adapters, source/team ownership metadata, all wildlife states and every
resource variant are excluded. Manual Farm/Sheep/fish Gather remains source-only.
Checkpoint validation rejects a Food area record targeting any of those sources
before restore mutates state; cargo may differ during a legitimate manual
handoff. Missing/null legacy Food intent stays source-only throughout recovery
and economy updates. No automatic policy is inferred from cargo or a current
legacy target. [Canonical intent contract](worker-resource-job-contract.md).

through the native server and assigns one Worker per seat. Food first exercises
real Stop with retained cargo and accepted manual successor/original replacements,

positive cargo, stops the server, restarts against the untouched checkpoint and
requires a strictly newer saved sequence before comparing the durable original
intent and temporary source. No serialized stock, bank, cargo or topology is
edited. Both Food and Stone finish with 24 drawn total, zero cargo, successors

not served/deployed or rendered gameplay evidence.
