## Ownership and stop conditions

The Coastal technical-art owner keeps unfinished steps on the [asset adoption ledger](asset-adoption-checklist.md). For a shared visual hook, agree on the exact factory/state/API boundary with its affected owner before editing; other streams keep moving. A missing runtime, actual ownership conflict, missing scoped publication authority or absent required review stops that dependent step only. Preserve the private packet, record the exact blocker and resume the same outcome when it clears.

Independent review is required by the [contributor planning guide](contributor-planning.md). Do not infer new permission holds from missing polish or pending unrelated QA, and do not bypass protections. Tooling/reference audits close with their own actual checks; they do not claim a fictitious game deployment.
