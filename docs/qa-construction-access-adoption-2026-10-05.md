## Observable contract

New construction and repair choose existing static-clear access cells outside
friendly ordinary land military accepted/pending endpoints. A fresh bounded
snapshot belongs to each command or synchronous construction phase. Actual
Worker poses also pass static-body and endpoint admission before productive work
or parking on completion; a safe assigned access cell alone is insufficient.
When a second Worker completes a site, another active builder retains its own
safe approach until it can legally become idle.

If every access cell is blocked or the query defers, the paid site, selected
Worker target/repair flag, remembered site IDs and area remain durable. There is
no invented route, false planner-pending flag, repayment, navigation mutation,
shove or military endpoint replacement. Availability retries run at most once
per second; shared route repair is enqueued only after a safe candidate exists.
An unsafe terminal leg suspends once by revision. Dynamic occupancy does not
consume or reset the existing three static attempts at the same site/navigation;
a new explicit command retains its normal fresh budget. Stop, Hold, replacement
and cancellation supersede transient waits, which reconstruct from normal saved
intent after cold recovery. Existing build/repair reach, rates and prices remain.

This query reserves ordinary friendly military endpoints, not their entire
routes or all current bodies. Workers, combat/held/persistent actors, enemies,
water and Sheep retain their existing policies. An already completed idle,
stopped or held builder keeps its pose: a subsequent military exact endpoint
still waits until that Worker receives its own departure command. The historical
[parked-builder audit](qa-construction-endpoint-contract-2026-10-05.md) remains
preserved; its military-first case now prevents the newly introduced conflict.

checkpoint job or payment is injected. Each observed selected-Worker substep

uses duplicate military endpoints to exercise the query budget; it does not

All-reserved builds wait 360 ticks with zero construction repair enqueues,

Wood cost once. Actual-pose cases deny productive progress while the approach
remains active. Cooperative completion preserves the other unsafe builder's
target until safe parking. Unselected Workers remain unchanged.
