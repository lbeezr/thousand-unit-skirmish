03 / A small shared vocabulary

Five button families.

Primary

One dominant next step in a decision group. Use brass, restrained texture and an inset rule.

Secondary

Alternatives remain easy to find. Pine surface, readable border, parchment label.

Quiet utility

Persistent tools and reading links. Keep a 44px target and a visible text cue.

Destructive

Use explicit consequence wording. Crimson is a flat lacquer surface; important loss also needs confirmation.

Selected choice

A celadon edge and check mark record a persistent choice. Pressed is a moment; selected is a state.

04 / Materials with purpose

Depth comes from separate layers.

Worked brass

Primary action and sparse edges. Keep the texture quiet under live labels. One shared surface asset.

Dark pine surface

Stable backing for controls. The same texture under a near-opaque wash gives depth without busy grain.

Living edges

Separate alpha foliage. Overlap the frame, clear the labels, shrink on mobile. Reserve it for entry and reading surfaces.

Landscape → material panel → brass rules → foliage → live controls. The decoration never receives pointer input.
