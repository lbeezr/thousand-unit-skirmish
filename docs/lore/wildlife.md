## Bellweather Sheep

![Bellweather animals in the selected ecology key](../art-direction/vaelora-v1/bellweather-ecology.png)

The cream-fleeced sheep pictured at middle left belongs to
[Bellweather's](regions/bellweather.md) pasture vocabulary. Its broad wool locks,
pale face, lateral ears and bare lower legs distinguish it from the slender
antlered deer and orange fox beside it. The painting shows no horns or harness.
Pasture and wool fit the region's existing working livelihoods; breed history,
husbandry customs and seasonal practices remain open.

The [first species design](../wildlife-bellweather-sheep.md) proposes a small flock
that players can claim, move and harvest for finite food. It also inventories
the ten inspected ecology keys and specifies future anatomy, perspective and
sprite work. Those original proposals are not established lore history;
current implementation and its limits are recorded in the linked delivery guide. The [Sheep angle-reference candidate](../art-direction/bellweather-sheep-reference-v3/README.md)
now carries approximate degree labels under the checked runtime convention.
The [first attempt](../art-direction/bellweather-sheep-reference-v1/README.md)
and [descriptive correction](../art-direction/bellweather-sheep-reference-v2/README.md)
remain in the evolution archive. Their printed camera, scale and pivot
registration are unverified; preserving these references adds no runtime change
and does not replace the later approved static views.

A [clean single-Sheep image input](../art-direction/bellweather-sheep-model-input-v1/README.md)
is prepared for the separately owned model-reference pilot; it is an approximate
front-three-quarter design source, not a generated model.
