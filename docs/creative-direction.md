Working direction, 14 October 2026. Creative stewardship: **Unify game art
direction and lore**, chat `01a13a8a-37a9-7812-800a-12e48c842b52`. This guide
connects the existing world, brand, art and voice references. Their detailed
contracts retain authority. It selects a continuation of the preserved direction;
new copy and the journey storyboard are editorial studies, not implemented screens
or additional canon. The [delivery record](art-direction/creative-atlas-v1/README.md#delivery-and-next-use)

## The creative spine

**Vaelora is a world worth tending, and a world worth contesting.**

Keep the existing public promise: **A living world. Yours to command.** The
sentence above is our internal test for it. A settlement should look useful
before it looks powerful. An army comes from people who have work, equipment,
neighbors and something to protect. Older power is tangible and sometimes
frightening; its history need not be fully explained to make a place compelling.
Command feels consequential because the world has a life beyond the battle.

This develops the [world overview](lore/world.md) and the author's sensibility
documented in the [voice bible](lore-voice-bible.md). It establishes no universal
war, ethical victory system, campaign premise or mandatory justification for a
friendly skirmish. The player can enjoy a match without reading the lore.

| Thread | What we should see, hear or read | Existing backing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inhabited craft | Stores with a working entrance; tools made for a task; water, food and shelter shaping a place | [Frontier architecture](lore/frontier-architecture.md), [Bellweather ecology](art-direction/vaelora-v1/bellweather-ecology.png) |
| Land with a character | Broad route shapes, habitat groups and settlements responding to terrain; a region recognizable before its name appears | [Regional atlas](art-direction/vaelora-v1/README.md), [regional environment kits](regional-environment-kits.md) |
| Companionable competence | People ready to do a particular job, with different priorities and occasional dry humor | [Prose voice](lore-voice-bible.md), [performance guide](civilization-voice-language-guide.md) |
| Power with a material consequence | A petrified interior, a resistant substance, an old instrument still needing care; wonder and dread with physical particulars | [Magic and materials](lore/magic.md), [Ru’Lora](lore/regions/ru-lora.md), [Pale Meridian](lore/regions/pale-meridian.md) |
| Command that earns trust | An order means the same thing in its label, sound and visible outcome; uncertainty and failures are stated plainly | [Gameplay contract](gameplay-command-observation-contract.md), [UI audio direction](ui-audio-direction.md) |

Warmth does not make every culture cheerful. Dread does not make every place
cursed. Leave room for an ordinary successful year, a competent disagreement
and a danger that receives no joke.

## One journey, changing attention

[Open the six-stage storyboard](art-direction/creative-atlas-v1/journey-storyboard.svg).
These are proposed compositions and editorial beats, not new loading delays,
runtime timings or evidence of gameplay. Each transition follows actual state.

| Stage | Player's immediate need | Treatment and voice | What carries forward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage / client | Recognize Vaelora and find a real entry | Inhabited landscape, pine, parchment, worked brass and bounded oak foliage. Inviting world prose beside literal New Game, Practice, Create Room, Join Room and Resume | Live wordmark, material restraint, a visible distinction between world concept art and game screenshots |
| Room / lobby | Know the place, rules, seats and next action | A quiet pine planning surface. Map identity and exact victory explanation outrank a vignette. Ready and Launch match remain literal. Region art, if introduced, is a labeled supporting reference | Same control shapes, pale text and focus logic; owner names and marks remain explicit |
| Mode / map decision | Understand the kind of contest | One concise rules sentence plus compatible map and opponent availability. Optional flavor follows the rule | Mode describes play; region describes setting; neither supplies a civilization or cosmology |
| Arrival / opening | Recognize the ground and begin work | Reveal the actual map and compact objective. Character identity moves into the selected portrait and short responses; foreground foliage recedes | Pine/brass welcome yields to the approved Obsidian command instrument; warm authored portraits join the two |
| Work / pressure / battle | Act and interpret consequences | Useful role silhouettes, readable paths and owner cues; practical routine acknowledgements. Stronger cues belong to observed urgent events | The same words and event meanings; quieter ornament as attention rises |
| Result / return | Understand the result and choose what follows | Actual outcome, reason and available replay/room action first. Let relief or loss have a brief tonal space without forced banter or invented statistics | Familiar frame and action hierarchy return; world reading stays optional |

The current entry uses **Bellweather concept art**, while fresh normal Skirmish
uses **Veyrholds · Terraced Vale**; the other reviewed human sizes also use
Veyrholds maps. This is a world portal leading to a particular battlefield.
Always name the selected battlefield before launch. Never imply that the hero
painting is Terraced Vale, or that its bridges and settlements exist on that map.
An authored regional scene is a later, separately verified use of the world.

## Materials, color and symbols

Use the [brand bible](vaelora-brand.md) for the client and
[interface design system](ui-design-system.md) for the battlefield. Both already
have user-selected elements. Cohesion comes from a controlled change of density
and purpose: do not repaint the approved Obsidian HUD pine or surround it with
the homepage's foliage simply to make screenshots match.

| Layer | Meaning | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand | This is Vaelora | Pine `#102c27`, parchment `#f3eddf`, brass `#d0b376`; live serif identity, rectangular frame and sparse crafted edges |
| Client interaction | What can I do; what state is this control in? | Brand semantic tokens and visible labels/shapes. Brass emphasizes the next major step; focus and selection have their own cues |
| Match interaction | What did I select; what is it doing; what can I command? | Approved Obsidian mineral panels, working sans/condensed type, expressive portraits and mint interaction accent. Retain its existing tokens and measured geometry |
| Ownership | Whose unit, building or objective is this? | Azure `#5aa7d7` + straight pennant/bar + square; Ember `#e67a5e` + forked pennant/split mark + diamond, with labels where needed |
| Region / craft | Where was this made, and what is it made from? | Ecology and culture materials from their own preserved references, without replacing owner or warning cues |

The decorative diamond separator in the portal is a small typographic ornament.
The Ember diamond is an ownership marker used with its team context. Keep
decorative diamonds away from seat rows, map legends and owner badges, where
they would compete with that meaning. The provisional V is navigation identity;
it is not an in-world nation's crest. Oaks belong to the selected Bellweather
entry treatment, not a universal religious emblem.

Resource, task and warning icons must follow their existing gameplay meanings
and retain text/accessibility names. A drawn grain/tree crest in an exploratory
settlement painting establishes no approved faction sigil. Boughward burgundy
is a craft accent, not Ember identity. A sun, moon or star motif needs an actual
lore context; it cannot silently settle survival, allegiance or a shared origin.

## Places and people stay distinct

Bellweather's working yards, cream masonry, honey timber and sage roofs suggest
cultivated warmth. The Veyrholds add exposed rock, conifers, sheltered interiors
and the cost of moving between heights. Underbough's roots, copper joints and
connected clearings suggest woodland craft. These are ways places and objects
respond to circumstances, not personality tests for every inhabitant.

Frontier remains the shared implemented gameplay civilization. Human and
Boughward are current roster presentations. The
[Boughward settlement boards](art-direction/civilization-settlements-v1/README.md)
remain exploratory; they do not replace its absent runtime building family.
Neither seat nor Azure/Ember color assigns a canonical accent, moral alignment
or permanent political membership. Keep ancestry, culture, residence, allegiance
and registered gameplay capabilities separate.

## Three speaking registers

| Speaker | Rule | Calibration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product / system | State the real action, condition and recovery. No fictional clerk speaks for a server error | **Ready**, **Launch match**, **Reconnecting**, and the actual rejection reason |
| Person / crew | Put the task or useful noun early. Let role and delivery supply character | Existing proposed Worker “What needs doing?” / “I'll mend it.”; Infantry “Together, then.”; Archer “Your mark?” |
| World / field note | A physical detail, a use, and a viewpoint or uncertainty. Reading is optional | New draft: “In the high holds, a sheltered doorway is part of the winter stores.” This is flavor calibration, not a selected local custom |

The [performance guide](civilization-voice-language-guide.md) owns audition scripts,
meaning IDs and binding limits; the [audio matrix](audio-event-asset-matrix.md)
owns actual recordings. Do not treat a proposed line as recorded or infer actor
identity from the present team/art association. Accepted-order speech follows
authoritative applied orders. Selection does not claim command acceptance;
an acknowledgement does not claim completion. Alerts use only the disclosed
event and do not invent a bearing, attacker or scouting observation.

Routine character direction is practical and companionable. Worker speaks as a
craftsperson; Archer is attentive to the mark; a siege crew handles mechanisms.
Boughward can explore coordinated woodland work without permanent growls or a
comic voice assigned to an entire ancestry. Vary priorities and delivery before
adding new invented words. Alarms remain literal; humor belongs in occasional
character material and optional prose, with space for danger to matter.

## Modes share the world and keep their own promise

The [mode registry contract](match-mode-contract.md) owns rules and eligibility.
These are creative emphases and copy briefs, not replacement descriptors.

| Existing path | Creative emphasis | Required disclosure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skirmish | Patient provision becomes maneuver, pressure and recovery | Recovery-aware elimination. Town Center destruction alone does not mean defeat. Fresh AI currently supports Tiny Terraced Vale only |
| Objective Control | A place matters enough to hold | The selected map's exact marked objectives, hold/deadline rules and rewards; no generic invented win timer |
| Bannerfall prototype | A compact contest with a recurring reinforcement beat | Original enemy Town Center is the stronghold; 15-second waves, supply cap and kill-based Rider unlock; human/Practice only |
| Practice | Space to inspect and rehearse | One human, no AI commander; selected rules remain explicit, including access to internal fixtures |
| Map Studio | The craft of making a contest understandable | Real authoring tools and valid map rules; a world map is not automatically a playable scenario |

Do not manufacture rivalries or named historical wars to justify every mode.
An authored story scenario can add a local reason through the lore wiki after
its setting and map are established. It must remain intelligible from the
objective alone. The Three Measures dispute remains working fiction, not the
secret history of Terraced Vale, Bannerfall or every water crossing.

## Rhythm and attention

Arrival invites; the lobby clarifies; the opening gives space to work; pressure
sharpens cues; the result explains and releases attention. This is a directional
arc, not a match-length claim or a scripted intensity curve. Actual player
decisions and authoritative events drive pacing.

Keep the selected 140ms client feedback and reduced-motion behavior. Do not add
cinematic waits to enter a room, a forced lore read before orders, constant
button shimmer or autoplay audio. Region music and ambience can maintain place
while command cues give short answers. The existing audio priority, cooldown,
mute/focus and interruption contracts govern overlap. A louder battle cannot
become a backlog of old spoken acknowledgements.

Riot's historical [clarity discussion](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/)
connects recognizable silhouettes, attention hierarchy and restrained visual/audio
noise. Our interpretation: keep the landscape particular, the role immediate and
the urgent event most salient. Its [Hextech UI article](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/under-hood-league-client%E2%80%99s-hextech-ui)
describes shared interface foundations. Our interpretation: carry common control
semantics across independently built screens, while varying presentation with
the player's task. Neither article supplies Vaelora's fiction or reusable art.

## Choosing the next creative change

Start with an actual player-facing seam and a source in the reference atlas.
State the desired recognition, select only the needed aspect, and preserve the
source's status. A culture sheet supplies materials and silhouette exploration;
it does not supply exact game scale, collision or canonical flags. A proposed
screen needs its state sequence; a source study does not establish appearance.

For a review, answer: does this belong to the same world; is its meaning clear
under pressure; does it preserve the selected direction and real capabilities;
and can someone trace it to inspectable art or writing? Record implementation
and ordinary-game evidence in the existing owning guide/QA, rather than treating
this creative reference as a second acceptance queue.

The [pregame-lobby implementation](qa-vaelora-lobby-2026-10-14.md) now carries the
client palette, brass action and control hierarchy into match preparation, with
full map naming and distinct written/team-shape seat identities. The original
source milestone remains dated; the new runtime increment has its own checks
and [state storyboard](art-direction/creative-atlas-v1/lobby-state-storyboard.svg).
The current creative owner retains clean-release, staging and qualified normal-room
appearance verification. The next independent creative seam is the result/return
language and identity; it must keep the actual reason and replay availability
clear without inventing a fictional history for a friendly match.
